# Submission Description
AgentStore OS is an AI-native storefront for founders to launch a microbusiness with paid digital services. It demonstrates a complete business loop: define services, accept Locus-centered payments, auto-fulfill with AI agents, and track revenue + order outcomes.

## Why this fits Week 4
This submission directly targets the LocusFounder track by keeping payment orchestration central to business operations.

## Why Locus is central
- Checkout is initiated through a Locus-oriented payment intent abstraction.
- Orders explicitly model payment status transitions (`pending -> processing -> paid/failed`).
- Fulfillment is intentionally gated on payment completion to reflect a real paid-work lifecycle.
